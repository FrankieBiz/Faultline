**Last checked:** October 26, 2026 (America/New_York)
**Official event page:** <https://events.cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon>
**Meta developer event page:** <https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon>

This is a working reference, not a replacement for the official rules. Recheck the official pages and Discord announcements for updates. The event page says dates may change at Meta's discretion.

## Event at a glance

- Ten-day, remote hackathon hosted by Meta and Cerebral Valley; participation is free for selected applicants.
- Event build window: October 26 through November 4, 2026. The official event page lists project submissions due **November 4 at 9:00 PM ET**.
- $1,000,000 total prize pool across two tracks.
- Themes:
  - **Build for Your World:** solve a problem in a domain you know firsthand.
  - **Push the Frontier:** apply models to challenges such as software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, or materials.
- Tracks:
  - **Indie developer:** individuals and students; creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works.
  - **Startup:** teams and founders building a real product; technical execution and production readiness.
- The official page says every project enters one track. [PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md) records the Indie Developer track for FaultLine; confirm it on the submission form.

## Judging rubric from the webinar

The same rubric applies in Stage 2 and the final round. Scores run from 0 to 10 per criterion.

| Criterion | Weight | Webinar description |
|---|---:|---|

## Avoid overdone project categories

The webinar slide says to skip these categories (not an exhaustive list):

- AI-generated medical advice, including mental-health advisors and skin-blemish detectors
- Basic RAG apps
- Simple image analyzers
- Streamlit apps
- Dashboards as the main feature
- “AI for Education” chatbots
- AI job-application screeners
- AI nutrition coaches
- Personality analyzers
- Sports analyzers or coaches

Treat this as guidance against generic concepts. If a concept overlaps, it needs a meaningfully differentiated problem, user, and execution.

Source: [`meta-provided/webinar/2026-10-26-anti-projects.png`](../meta-provided/webinar/2026-10-26-anti-projects.png).

## Strategy implications

1. Start with a specific, firsthand problem and a clearly defined beneficiary. Impact is the largest scoring category.
2. Choose a concept that can be built and demonstrated reliably inside the event window.
3. Use Meta tooling in a way central to the experience, not as a thin API wrapper.
4. Show technical craft and a stable, easy-to-understand demo. Record a short demo video.
5. Keep a dated source trail and verify deadlines, access, and credit amounts against official materials.
