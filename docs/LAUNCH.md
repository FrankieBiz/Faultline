# FaultLine launch materials

Drafts only. Publish after a live Meta repair, public-demo hardening, and a recorded video are verified. Do not expose the local control plane directly.

## The shareable demonstration

Open with the checkout failure, rather than a title card. Show the actual generated diff, a candidate that passes independent checks, then the real trace/report. The memorable claim is inspectable verification. Do not substitute a timer-driven “healed” animation, a fabricated receipt, or an unmeasured recovery time.

A useful second clip shows a proposed patch rejected by policy or a regression. Identify whether the proposal was authored for the test or generated by the model. A rejection is valuable only if viewers can see why it was rejected.

## Show HN draft

Title: **Show HN: FaultLine — browser reproduction and verified source-repair candidates**

First comment should explain the seeded scope, exact edit boundary, Linux process containment, external verification, and what still needs testing. Invite readers to run the suite and challenge the assertions. Do not imply deployment, certified security, quantified customer savings, or general repair reliability.

## Release requirements

- A readable video showing a live Meta attempt and matching retained evidence.
- A confirmed firsthand problem statement from the builder and feedback from intended users.
- Repeated trials with successes, rejections, and failures reported together.
- A second actual defect or holdout case, rather than more simulated scenarios.
- A deployed public experience with an explicit read-only/replay mode or separately reviewed access and resource controls.
- Verified submission and social links, a source link, and accessible captions.

No distribution, engagement count, or viral outcome is promised. These materials are prepared for owner review; no posts or submissions are sent automatically.
